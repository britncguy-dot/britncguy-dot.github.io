    How to use it

    Quit staring at chord sheets. Put in the progression.

    Use the song you are working on. FretFlow takes a lot of the thinking away by showing nearby ways to move between chords, so your understanding grows while you practice.

    1. Pick the mode. Use Progression for songs, Pentatonic for runs, or Chord Tones to study the 1, 3, and 5.
    2. Choose your labels. Switch between note names, degrees, or do re mi until the fretboard makes sense.
    3. Read the colored circles. Gold is 1, green is 3, and blue is 5. Open-string notes sit before the nut.
    4. Move slowly. Press Play, say the numbers out loud, then use Next to find the closest clean grip.
