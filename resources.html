No-FOMO Trader Resources

Use these before and after the trading day. The goal is simple: judge the conditions, define the risk, and review the decision honestly.

Pre-Trade Checklist

  • What kind of market is this: trend, range, chop, or news-driven?
  • What level matters most right now?
  • What setup am I actually waiting for?
  • Where is my stop before entry?
  • Am I calm enough to pass if the trade is not clean?

Setup Quality Score

  • Trend alignment: 0-10
  • Level clarity: 0-10
  • Defined risk: 0-10
  • Entry patience: 0-10
  • No-FOMO discipline: 0-10

Clean Trade Review

  • Did I respect market conditions?
  • Did I wait for a real setup?
  • Did I enter with defined risk?
  • Did I avoid chasing?
  • What did this trade teach me?

Journal Questions

  • Where did I feel urgency today?
  • What level did I respect well?
  • What trade did I pass, and was that the right call?
  • Did I trade my plan or my emotion?
  • What is one rule I will carry into tomorrow?
