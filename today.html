The No-FOMO Brief: Strength Still Has To Hold

A No-FOMO Trader daily learning brief about respecting strength without chasing a reclaim after it has already run.

June 12, 2026 Education only No signals
The No-FOMO Trader Market Discipline

Source: SPY 15 minute market map

Market Context

We are walking into a different kind of morning than yesterday.

Yesterday the chart was still asking for a reclaim. This morning, they have already pushed SPY back through the lower reclaim path. Price is working above 732.14 and 735.53, with 739.26 sitting overhead as the next proof area.

That is strength, and we should respect it. But strength is not the same thing as permission to chase.

This is the kind of morning where the chart can make us feel late. They push it up, the candles look strong, and the mind starts saying, "I should have already been in." That is exactly where the No-FOMO process has to slow us down.

We can respect the move without hero trading the reclaim test.

Key line:

Above 732.14, the major reclaim test is active. Above 735.53, price has repaired more of the prior damage. The next overhead proof area is 739.26. If price loses 735.53, the move starts getting less clean; below 732.14, the reclaim is failing.

Market Conditions Check

  • Trend clarity: improved, but still near an overhead test.
  • Level respect: 735.53 needs to hold if the reclaim is going to stay clean.
  • Pressure zone: 739.26 is the next overhead proof area.
  • No-FOMO meter note: a strong move can still be a bad chase if our entry comes late.

Technical Map

SPY:

  • active reclaim area: 732.14.
  • current hold-check area: 735.53.
  • next overhead proof area: 739.26.
  • lower fallback guides: 729.42, 728.29, and 727.39.
  • deeper danger area: 725.42, then 725.33.

QQQ:

  • confirmation role: QQQ needs to support the SPY reclaim instead of fighting it.
  • danger zone: assuming one strong push means the whole day is clean.
  • confidence rule: if SPY and QQQ do not agree, we lower confidence and study instead of forcing.

One Setup To Study

Study the first pullback after the reclaim.

The weak question is:

Can we jump in before it breaks higher?

The better question is:

If they pull it back, does 735.53 or 732.14 hold as support with risk we can actually define?

That question keeps us from turning a strong candle into an emotional decision.

No-FOMO Rule

Do not chase the reclaim after it has already run. Let the retest prove whether the reclaim is real.

Journal Question

Did we respect strength without turning it into urgency?
