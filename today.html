The No-FOMO Brief Sample

1. Market Context

Before looking for trades, ask what kind of market you are in. Is price trending cleanly, chopping around a level, reacting to news, or fading moves quickly? The condition decides how aggressive the plan can be.

A mixed market does not mean do nothing. It means require cleaner proof, smaller size, and a more obvious invalidation point.

2. Technical Map

  • Mark the prior day high and low before the open.
  • Mark the pre-market range and where price is accepting or rejecting.
  • Watch whether SPY and QQQ agree or tell different stories.
  • Avoid the middle of a messy range unless the risk point is clear.

3. Setup To Study

Study the first clean pullback after a confirmed move. The goal is not to catch the first candle. The goal is to wait for the market to prove whether buyers or sellers can defend a level.

4. No-FOMO Rule

If the trade only looks good because it is already moving without me, I wait for the next clean setup.

5. Journal Prompt

Where did I feel urgency today, and did that urgency come from a real setup or from wanting to be included in the move?
