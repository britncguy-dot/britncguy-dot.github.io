The No-FOMO Brief: A Bounce Inside Damage Is Still Damage

A No-FOMO Trader daily learning brief about distinguishing a lower bounce inside damage from a true repair.

June 24, 2026 Education only No signals
The No-FOMO Trader Market Discipline

Market Context

SPY is not waking up in a healthy area this morning. It is trading deep under the larger repair map and working inside a lower intraday pressure stack.

That matters because a bounce from the lower area can look exciting before it proves anything. When a chart is still buried under broken structure, we do not give every green candle the same respect as a true reclaim. We want to see price recover one level, hold it, and then keep proving itself through the next layer overhead.

A bounce inside damage is movement. A repaired chart is evidence.

One Setup To Study

Study the first real stair-step reclaim after a hard breakdown.

The weak question is:

Can we buy just because the chart looks stretched?

The better question is:

Can SPY reclaim 734.72, then 736.27, then 739.26 with actual holds instead of quick stalls?

That question keeps us focused on sequence instead of emotion.

No-FOMO Rule

Do not confuse a lower bounce with a finished repair.
