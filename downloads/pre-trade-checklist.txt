THE NO-FOMO TRADER
Pre-Trade Checklist

Education only. Not financial advice or trade advice.

1. Market Condition
- Trend
- Range
- Chop
- News-driven

2. Key Level
- What level matters most right now?
- Is price respecting it or ignoring it?

3. Setup
- What exact setup am I waiting for?
- What confirms it?
- What invalidates it?

4. Risk
- Where is the stop before entry?
- Is the risk amount acceptable?
- Can I take the loss without moving the stop?

5. Emotion
- Am I calm enough to pass?
- Am I entering because of a plan or because I feel late?

Final rule:
If the trade is unclear, the trade is not owed my money.
