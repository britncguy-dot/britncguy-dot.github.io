THE NO-FOMO TRADER
Clean Trade Review

Education only. Not financial advice or trade advice.

Trade reviewed:
Date:
Market condition:

Before entry:
- Did I define the key level?
- Did I know what would prove me wrong?
- Did I define the stop before entry?
- Did I accept the risk before entry?

During trade:
- Did I follow the plan?
- Did I chase?
- Did I move the stop emotionally?
- Did I size correctly?

After trade:
- Was this a clean decision?
- What did I do well?
- What needs improvement?
- What rule carries into tomorrow?

Important:
A winning chase can still be a bad decision. A losing planned trade can still be clean.
