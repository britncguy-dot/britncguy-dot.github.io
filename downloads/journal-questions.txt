THE NO-FOMO TRADER
Journal Questions

Education only. Not financial advice or trade advice.

Daily prompts:

1. What kind of market did I face today?

2. What level mattered most?

3. Where did I feel urgency?

4. Did I wait for my setup or chase movement?

5. Did I define risk before entry?

6. What trade did I pass on, and was that wise?

7. Did I trade the plan or the emotion?

8. What is one rule I will carry into the next session?
