How the meter works

Manual inputs, disciplined output.

The free meter does not pull live market data yet. It is a learning tool that turns your own read of trend, levels, pressure, and chase risk into a calmer readiness score.

  • Trend clarity and key levels raise the score when the chart has structure.
  • Volatility and news pressure lower the score when conditions are harder to trust.
  • Urge to chase lowers the score because emotion is part of trade quality.
  • Yellow is useful. It means be selective, not scared.

Market conditions first

A good setup still needs the right environment.

The No-FOMO process teaches traders to judge the market before judging the trade. A clean-looking entry can still be low quality if the broader conditions are messy.

01

02

03

Discipline

Entry planned before the move, stop respected, position size controlled, emotion checked.

Who it helps

For traders who know the feeling.

You watch a move start without you. The candle keeps going. You feel late, but you still want in. That is where many accounts get hurt.

This brief is for the everyday trader who wants to slow the decision down, respect levels, and trade from a plan instead of pressure.

Common traps we train against

  • Chasing a candle because it feels like the last chance.
  • Trading the middle of a range with no clean risk point.
  • Confusing market movement with a real setup.
  • Revenge trading after one frustrating loss.
  • Moving stops because being wrong feels uncomfortable.
