Market conditions first

Who it helps

For traders who know the feeling.

You watch a move start without you. The candle keeps going. You feel late, but you still want in. That is where many accounts get hurt.

This brief is for the everyday trader who wants to slow the decision down, respect levels, and trade from a plan instead of pressure.

Common traps we train against

  • Chasing a candle because it feels like the last chance.
  • Trading the middle of a range with no clean risk point.
  • Confusing market movement with a real setup.
  • Revenge trading after one frustrating loss.
  • Moving stops because being wrong feels uncomfortable.
