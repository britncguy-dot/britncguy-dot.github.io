The Mower Rule: Stop Before You Trade

Opening Read

A pretty Friday can make the mind want to do two things at once: get the outside work done and keep one eye on the market. That is a common trading trap. The phone makes the market feel close, but close does not mean clear.

The No-FOMO rule for today is simple:

Do not trade from motion. Stop first. Look second. Decide third.

If you are mowing, working, driving, helping family, or doing anything that splits your attention, the market can wait for a stopped break.

Market Conditions Note

Yesterday carried the heavier macro punch with inflation and growth data. Today is more of a follow-through and discipline day. The calendar still has items that can move intraday tone, including trade balance, wholesale inventories, Fed speakers, and Chicago PMI.

Early snapshot: SPY and QQQ are green, with QQQ leading stronger than DIA. That can look like risk appetite, but the first green read is not the same thing as a clean trade.

The job is not to chase green.

The job is to ask whether the move still respects structure after the first excitement fades.

SPY / QQQ Map

For SPY and QQQ, keep the map simple:

  • Opening range high: first resistance test.
  • Opening range low: first support test.
  • VWAP area: decision zone, not automatic entry.
  • QQQ vs SPY: check whether tech leadership holds or fades.
  • DIA lag: note whether broader market participation is thin.

    • If QQQ keeps leading while SPY confirms, the market may be more constructive.

    If QQQ runs ahead while SPY hesitates and DIA stays flat, be careful calling it broad strength.

Market Conditions Check

  • Trend clarity: early green tone, but still needs structure confirmation.
  • Level respect: judge the opening range, VWAP, and first clean pullback.
  • Volatility pressure: lighter than yesterday's data cluster, but Friday and month-end behavior can still get sloppy.
  • Macro/news pressure: watch the 9:45 AM ET Chicago PMI reaction and Fed speaker headlines.
  • No-FOMO meter note: yellow. Study first, act only if the setup is clean enough to explain.

    • Setup To Study

    Study the first clean pullback after the early green push.

    Do not treat it as an automatic entry. Make it answer questions:

  • Did price pull back calmly, or did it fall apart?
  • Did SPY and QQQ agree?
  • Did VWAP hold, reclaim, or reject?
  • Did the bottom indicator support the move, or warn that the energy was fading?
  • Could the trade be written down in one plain sentence before entering?

    • If the answer is muddy, skip it.

    Score Coach Rule

    The mower rule:

    If I cannot stop, write the setup, and explain the risk, I do not touch the trade.

    Movement is for yard work.

    Stillness is for trading decisions.

    Journal Question

    Did I want to check the market because there was a clean setup, or because I was afraid something was happening without me?

    No-FOMO Life Note

    Outside work teaches a trading lesson: finish the lane you are in.

    Mow when it is time to mow.

    Study when it is time to study.

    Trade only when the plan is clear and your attention is fully there.
