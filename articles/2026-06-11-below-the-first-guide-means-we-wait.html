No-FOMO Trader Brief

A No-FOMO Trader daily learning brief about waiting for SPY to reclaim the first guide before trusting a bounce.

June 11, 2026 Education only No signals
The No-FOMO Trader Daily brief

Source: SPY 15 minute market map

Market Context

We are walking into the morning with SPY below the first reclaim guide. That matters because below the guide, the chart can move around and still not be clean.

The first guide is 727.39. Until price reclaims that area and holds it, we do not have to treat every green candle like proof. We can let them try first. If they prove it, we study the next level. If they fail, we saved ourselves from chasing.

This is one of those mornings where the market may feel loud, but the job is simple:

Let price come back to the map before we trust it.

Market Conditions Check

  • Trend clarity: weak until the first guide is reclaimed.
  • Level respect: 727.39 is the first proof line.
  • Pressure zones: 728.29, then 729.42, then 732.14.
  • No-FOMO meter note: a bounce from a low is not the same as a reclaim.

One Setup To Study

Study the first reclaim-and-hold attempt after a weak open.

The weak question is:

Can we catch the low before everyone else?

The better question is:

Did price reclaim the guide, hold it, and give us a clean risk point?

That question keeps us from turning hope into a trade idea.

No-FOMO Rule

Do not buy the bounce because it is green. Wait for the reclaim to prove itself.

Journal Question

Did we want the trade because price proved the level, or because we were afraid the move would leave without us?
