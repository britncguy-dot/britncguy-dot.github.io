The No-FOMO Brief: Let Support Prove Itself

A No-FOMO Trader daily learning brief about waiting for a support shelf to prove itself before trusting a bounce.

June 17, 2026 Education only No signals
The No-FOMO Trader Daily brief

Market Context

SPY is testing support after pulling back from the upper part of the recent move.

The first area to respect is 749.00. When price pulls back into a prior support shelf, we do not automatically call it bullish and we do not automatically call it broken. We let price prove whether buyers are still willing to defend that area.

That is the difference between guessing and reading structure. A bounce near support can look exciting, but a bounce is not proof by itself. We want to see price stabilize, reclaim nearby levels, and hold above them long enough to show that the move has real support behind it.

When price is sitting on support, the job is not to predict the bounce. The job is to watch whether the level holds.

One Setup To Study

Study the first clean support test after a pullback from the upper area.

The weak question is:

Can we guess the bounce before it proves itself?

The better question is:

Does 749.00 hold, and can price reclaim nearby structure with risk we can actually define?

That question keeps us from turning a support test into a prediction.

No-FOMO Rule

Support is not permission. Support is a place where price has to prove itself.

Journal Question

Did we wait for the shelf to prove support, or did we treat the first bounce like confirmation?
